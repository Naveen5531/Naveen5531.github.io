Punch Privacy Policy

Punch attendance app and staff portal · SN Logistics · Last updated 4 November 2026

Punch is used by your employer to record attendance, leave and pay. This page explains what Punch collects, why, where it is kept and what you can ask for. Your employer is responsible for your data (the "Data Fiduciary" under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023).

What Punch collects

LocationYour phone's location when you check in and check out, and while you are checked in. If you turn on Auto check-in, Punch also checks your location in the background (including when the app is closed) to check you in and out at your workplace and to log work trips. Punch does not track you when you are not checked in and Auto check-in is off.
AttendanceCheck-in and check-out times, hours worked, attendance marks, leave, comp off and the requests you send.
Profile and payName, employee ID, designation, grade, workplace, weekly offs, salary set-up, payslips, offer and salary letters, and the bank / PAN / UAN details you or HR enter for the payslip.
App settingsSaved places, trips, expenses you add, theme and festival settings.
Sign-inYour login name, or your Gmail address if you sign in with Google.

Punch does not read your contacts, photos, messages or calls, and does not show ads or sell data.

Why it is used

  • To record attendance and work hours, and to check that check-ins happen at the workplace.
  • To work out leave balances, salary and payslips, and to prepare HR letters.
  • To let HR approve your requests and to keep your data when you change phones.

Your location is used only for attendance and work trips, and only by your employer.

Where it is kept and who sees it

  • Data is stored on your phone and in your employer's Google Firebase database (Google Cloud, Mumbai region where set up so), protected by sign-in and access rules.
  • You see your own data. HR sees attendance, requests and profiles of employees. Salary is visible only to HR head, HR manager and admins.
  • Data is not shared with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

How long it is kept

Detailed check-in locations are kept on the phone for about 3 months and trips for 1 year. Attendance, pay and HR records are kept as long as your employer must keep employment and payroll records under law, and then deleted.

Your choices and rights

  • You can turn off Auto check-in in Punch › Settings at any time, and you can turn off location for Punch in your phone settings (check-in will then not work).
  • You can ask your employer to see, correct or delete your data, to withdraw consent, or to name someone to act for you, and you can raise a complaint. If you are not satisfied, you can approach the Data Protection Board of India.

Contact

For questions or requests about your data, contact your HR department.
