How Punch works

From the phone to the payslip, one record

Each step lands in the same database the staff portal reads, so HR never re-types attendance.

01

Check in at work

The app checks the phone is within the workplace radius, then records in and out times with location.

02

Ask from the app

Leave, comp off and attendance corrections go to HR with a reason. Comp off is used up as soon as it is applied.

03

HR decides live

Requests appear in the portal the moment they are sent. Approve and the employee's app updates straight away.

04

Payslip every month

Pay days and salary are worked out from attendance. Employees see their net pay; HR sees the full working.
